## DoorDash Promo Code Troubleshooting

If a DoorDash code is rejected, check the offer terms before trying another random code.

### “This promotion is not available for your account”

The code may be limited to first-time customers, selected users, or a specific campaign. Creating multiple accounts to repeat a new-customer offer can also violate promotion terms. DoorDash’s general promotional terms say that prior use of a name, email, phone number, payment card, or linked account can disqualify a customer from a new-user offer.

### “Minimum subtotal not met”

The minimum usually applies to the food or product subtotal, not the total after taxes and fees. Adding delivery fees to the cart total will not necessarily help you qualify.

### The discount disappears at checkout

Some offers are limited to selected restaurants, order categories, or times. Others cannot be combined with DashPass, credits, gift cards, or another promotion.

### Cashback is missing

First check that you started from ShopBack, completed the purchase in one session, and did not use an excluded voucher or promo code. ShopBack says a purchase may be tracked at a different initial rate and adjusted after DoorDash confirms the transaction.

A missing cashback entry is not proof that the order failed permanently, especially when the expected tracking window is up to about 2 days. If the purchase still does not appear after that period, keep your receipt and order details for a ShopBack support request.
